I. Between all color channels, the green channel most resembles a gray-scale conversion of the original image. \\

---\\

II. This is because the human eye is more sensitive to green light compared to red and blue light. \\
The luminance component of an image, which contributes to perceived brightness, is heavily influenced by the green channel. \\
As a result, when converting an image to grayscale, the green channel often provides a more accurate representation of the \\
original scene's brightness and detail.\\

---\\

III. This matters since green channel has the smallest difference in pixel values between any of the images usually. \\
Cameras have have double the number of green pixels compared to red and blue pixels, which is why the green channel is more prevalent and widely used in image processing.

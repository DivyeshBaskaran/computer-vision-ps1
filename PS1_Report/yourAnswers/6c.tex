I. The Green channel looks more noisy to me.\\

---\\

II. This is because the human eye is more sensitive to green light than blue light. \\
The human eye has more green cones than blue cones, which means that we are better at detecting variations in green light. \\
As a result, noise in the green channel is more noticeable to us than noise in the blue channel.\\
Additionally, cameras are often designed to be more sensitive to green light, which can also contribute to the perception of more noise in the green channel.\\
Camera sensors often have double the number of green pixels compared to red and blue pixels, which can also make noise in the green channel more noticeable.\\
